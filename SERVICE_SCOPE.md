# Restaurant Website Customization Service

## Included

- Customization for one restaurant deployment.
- Restaurant name, address, hours, phone, WhatsApp, email, colors, and logo.
- Customer-provided menu items, prices, and licensed images.
- Chatbot knowledge context for the approved menu, hours, services, and reservation process.
- Optional Gemini proxy configuration using the customer's API account.
- Deployment on one compatible customer-provided hosting environment.
- Basic launch and security checklist.
- Seven calendar days of reasonable configuration support after delivery.

## Excluded

- Hosting, domain, API, messaging, email, or other third-party fees.
- Ongoing content updates or server administration.
- Online payment processing, POS integration, delivery-platform integration, or custom back-office software.
- Legal, privacy, accessibility, food-safety, or allergen certification.
- Ownership or licensing of customer-provided photos, logos, menu text, or reviews.

## Customer responsibilities

The customer supplies accurate, approved content and confirms the right to use all provided assets. The customer also supplies compatible hosting and temporary limited-access credentials. Temporary access should be revoked after delivery.
